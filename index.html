About Us

Different businesses.
One identity.

Spenpy Enterprises connects distinct services under one recognisable brand, giving customers a simple way to discover, choose and get in touch. From printed ideas, to new destinations, to African cuisine — each business has its own character while sharing the Spenpy identity.

Whether you need branded merchandise, a guided trip across West Africa, a table of authentic dishes, a reliable sourcing partner or everyday essentials, you start in one place and we point you the right way.

  • Five specialised businesses, each with its own expertise

  • A single point of contact in Accra, always a message away
