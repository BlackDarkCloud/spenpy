how it works

From brief to finished job in four steps.

1

Brief

Tell us what you need: a sketch, a logo, a size, a quantity. Message us on WhatsApp, call, or walk in.

2

Design

We prepare the artwork and agree the look with you before anything goes to print.

3

Print

Digital, offset, large format, DTF or engraving. Each job goes to the right machine.

4

Finish

Cutting, pressing, stitching, binding and packing. Then it is ready for you.
